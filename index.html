About Roto Moulders

Roto Moulders Limited was incorporated in Kenya in 1991, and has since become a leader in the field of rotational moulding in East Africa. Located in Nairobi, off Enterprise Road, in the Industrial Area, we house our factory and sales offices. As a full-fledged member of the ARM-USA, Roto Moulders has the largest range of water tanks in East Africa.

We are water tank manufacturers in Kenya, producing cylindrical water storage tanks from 100 litres to 24,000 litres, rectangular loft tanks from 230 to 920 litres, bell-shaped underground tanks from 1000 litres to 6000 litres, and septic tanks from 1700 litres to 6000 litres.

Frequently asked questions

What water tank sizes does Roto Tanks sell?

Roto Moulders makes cylindrical water storage tanks from 100 to 24,000 litres, rectangular loft tanks from 230 to 920 litres, bell-shaped underground tanks from 1,000 to 6,000 litres and septic tanks from 1,700 to 6,000 litres. The price list on this page shows tanks from 1,000L to 24,000L.

How much does a 5,000 litre water tank cost in Kenya?

The 5,000L Deluxe Cylindrical Tank is listed at KSh 24,500 on this page, and the smallest tank listed, the 1,000L Deluxe Cylindrical Tank, is KSh 6,500. Prices can change, so our team confirms the final price with you on WhatsApp.

How do I order?

Press Add to cart on each product you want, then open your cart and choose Order on WhatsApp. To buy one product, press Order now. WhatsApp opens with your products, quantities and total price already written, ready to send to 0733 355 444.

Where is Roto Moulders located?

Roto Moulders is based in Nairobi, off Enterprise Road in the Industrial Area, where the factory and sales offices are.

Do Roto tanks come with a warranty?

Our tanks are listed with a 30-year warranty. Ask our team on WhatsApp or by phone for the warranty terms that apply to your tank.

Do you sell products other than water tanks?

Yes. Besides water tanks we sell planters, dustbins and garbage bins, road barriers and traffic cones, PE manholes and pit latrine slabs, plastic pallets, cooler boxes and school desks.
