Roto Tanks website
==================

Upload the whole folder to the root of your hosting (the folder that holds index.html).

Pages
- /                  Home (all 39 products, FAQ, contact)
- /water-tanks/  /agriculture/  /bins/  /road-safety/  /sanitation/  /miscellaneous/
- 404.html           Page not found

Shopping
- Add to cart collects several products and sends one WhatsApp message with all items and the total.
- Order now sends a single product straight to WhatsApp (no form).
- Phone and WhatsApp number: 0733 355 444 (+254733355444).

SEO files
- robots.txt, sitemap.xml and the social share image (assets/og-image.jpg) assume the live address https://rototanks.online/
  If your domain is different, change it in robots.txt, sitemap.xml and every canonical / og:url / JSON-LD link.

After going live
1. Add the site in Google Search Console and submit https://rototanks.online/sitemap.xml
2. Claim or update your Google Business Profile (same name, address and phone as the site).
3. Replace the product photos that are linked from toptank.com with your own, saved in assets/.
